## Follow-up Fix Report (Status: RESOLVED)

All 19 approved items were remediated:

1. **Items 1, 2, 3 (R-36, R-38)**: Removed all fabricated security badges ("Secure 256-Bit SSL"), fake 24/7 support claims, and marketing bullets from customer sign-in.
2. **Items 4, 5, 6 (R-39, R-40)**: Added `aria-hidden="true"` to all decorative RemixIcons in input groups and buttons; removed invalid `role="switch"` from remember checkbox; added explicit width/height dimensions to logo.
3. **Items 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 (R-01, R-04, R-08, R-09, R-12)**: Removed hardcoded dark gradient, floating shield badge, lightning icon, circular feature icon stack, arrow `&larr;`, duplicate app capsule badge, and replaced heavy shadows with `--xshop-shadow-sm`.
4. **Items 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 (R-05, R-11, R-16, R-29, AGENTS.md tokens & localization)**: Replaced cluttered 2-column showcase with centered, clean, single-card layout; removed `rounded-pill` from action buttons; cleaned marketing buzzwords to direct labels; normalized dev buttons to `btn-outline-secondary`; localized toast messages via data attributes; used `--xshop-*` tokens.
